Gnomepit: Adena checks

These checks run against the local chain started by make dev at the repository's root. Fund your Adena account there first with make fund ADDRESS=<your address>. Each check writes its outcome under its button.

0. Connect

1. The root signature is stable

Adena signs the same text twice. The two signatures must be equal, here and on another device with the same wallet.

2. Adena creates a session

Approve one signature and one transaction. The page then posts a challenge signed by its own session key, with no pop-up, and the arena must list your address as its challenger.

3. Adena signs a move

The fallback: Adena signs and sends one challenge itself.

4. Adena revokes a dead session

Approve up to two transactions. The page posts a challenge through a session and withdraws it, which leaves that session dead, then posts again: the second approval revokes the dead session and creates the next one.
